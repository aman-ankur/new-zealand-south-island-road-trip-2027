The road-trip logic

The hardest road days become sightseeing days. Two nights on the northern West Coast create a proper Paparoa day; two-night stays also protect Wānaka, Milford Sound, Queenstown and Aoraki/Mount Cook. The final Christchurch night removes pressure from flight day.

Day 3 · Thursday, 11 March

Full Punakaiki and Paparoa day

~100 km return~1½–1¾ hr drivingNo hotel move

This is why the second West Coast night is valuable. Time Pancake Rocks for the better tide, then choose one or two light Paparoa walks without packing the car.

Best balanced plan

🌊
Pancake Rocks and Blowholes

Easy paved loop; high tide and westerly swell usually make the strongest blowholes. DOC currently charges for Dolomite Point parking, so recheck 2027 rates.

🌴
Pororari River short walk

Walk a 30–60 minute out-and-back beneath limestone cliffs and nīkau palms. Skip in heavy rain, when lower sections can become impassable.

🌿
Truman Track

A short rainforest-to-coast walk. Add it only if conditions and energy remain comfortable after Pororari.

Second night

📍
Return to Point Elizabeth Heights

No packing or hotel change. Refuel in Greymouth and prepare for the glacier-country transfer.

🌅
Coast sunset

Use a signed beach access near the accommodation rather than adding another long drive.

Day trip: Point Elizabeth / Greymouth → Punakaiki → Pororari River → Truman Track → base · roughly 100 km / ~1½–1¾ hr wheels moving
